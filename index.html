HOW OUR SNEAKERS ARE CRAFTED

Engineered with precision 3D CAD modeling, high-rebound Nitro foam, and Italian leather stitching.

🖥️
1. 3D Generative Ergonomic Design
Every sneaker starts with high-resolution 3D CAD geometry modeling to calculate optimal foot pressure distribution and drag coefficients.
🧪
2. Vulcanized Nitro Foam Molding
Our midsoles feature high-energy Nitro gas injection molding for maximum energy return and ultra-lightweight cushioning.
🧵
3. Italian Leather & Mesh Stitching
Uppers are hand-cut from premium full-grain Italian leather and double-stitched with breathable high-tenacity woven mesh.
⚡
4. Aerodynamic & Traction Certification
Each batch undergoes high-friction outsole testing, impact absorption analysis, and 360° quality verification before store delivery.

📖 Brand Origin & History

🚀 HOW THE BRAND WAS MADE

NEON SNEAKER STORE was founded in 2026 by lead 3D Web Engineer & UI Architect Atharva Ghugare. Frustrated by traditional 2D static sneaker websites where buyers couldn't see authentic leather textures or sole thickness before ordering, Atharva pioneered real-time WebGL 3D rendering to bring full 360° interactive digital twin customization to sneakerheads worldwide.

💡 HOW THE NAME "NEON SNEAKER" CAME TO BE

The name "NEON SNEAKER" was inspired by the vibrant midnight neon lightscapes of Tokyo’s Harajuku district and Mumbai’s Cyber BKC.

"NEON" represents high-energy Nitro gas cushioning, vibrant multi-panel leather colorways, and futuristic 3D Web illumination that pops on human eyes.

"SNEAKER" honors authentic high-top Jordan & low-top Puma performance heritage crafted for agility and street style.

🌟 OUR CORE PHILOSOPHY

1. Authentic 3D Precision
Real GLB CAD geometry matching every millimeter of authentic footwear.
2. High-Contrast Low-Vision Accessibility

Bridging online 3D Web shopping with physical flagship store vaults in Mumbai, Tokyo, NYC, and London.
